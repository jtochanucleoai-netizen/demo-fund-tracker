How the tracker works

  • Purchase. The fund buys the 13 holdings of the group's final order at the official closing price of 27 October 2026, the day the portfolio was presented. No broker account is used.
  • Costs. A 0.5% currency-conversion fee (a typical retail broker rate) on every purchase in dollars, pounds or zloty, and on converting dividends. Where costs left cash below zero, the fund bought one share fewer of TotalEnergies, the holding with the lowest expected return.
  • Dividends. Credited to cash on the ex-dividend date, after withholding tax at source (US 15%, France 12.8%, Netherlands 15%, Germany 26.4%, Poland 19%, UK 0%). Portuguese tax is not deducted.
  • Benchmark. iShares Core MSCI World (IWDA, Amsterdam, in euros), scaled to the same €50,000 start.
  • Updates. Automatic, twice per weekday: after the European close and after the US close (scheduled runs on GitHub, which can start up to an hour late). Between updates the figures show the last prices fetched.
  • Changes. Phase-2 alterations (around 24 November) are recorded as sales and purchases at that day's close, with the same costs.
Prices: Yahoo Finance (delayed). Educational project, not investment advice.
